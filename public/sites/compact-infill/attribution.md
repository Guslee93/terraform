## Roads and trees

© OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL 1.0), from the same extract as the buildings.
Roads are `highway` centrelines clipped to the terrain extent; widths are illustrative
values by highway class, not measured carriageways. Tunnels and area-tagged ways are
omitted; a covered passage through a building (`tunnel=building_passage`) is kept as a
street. Where the extract is missing a node the way is split there rather than joined
across the gap.

Trees are mapped OSM positions: `natural=tree` nodes, and `natural=tree_row` lines with a
tree placed every 8 m along the row. The positions are the mappers' own; the sizes and
tints are illustrative and vary by position, so no species, height or canopy data is
implied. Where OSM has no tree, none is drawn — the ground is left as it is mapped.

## Land cover

© OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL 1.0), from the same cached extract.
Water: `natural=water`, `waterway=riverbank`, `landuse=reservoir|basin`, and
`waterway=river|canal|stream` drawn as illustrative ribbons (10 m, 6 m and 4 m wide).
Wood: `natural=wood`, `landuse=forest`. Scrub: `natural=scrub`, `natural=heath`,
`landuse=scrub`. Wetland: `natural=wetland`, `landuse=wetland`. Bare ground:
`natural=bare_rock|sand|scree|shingle`. Cemetery: `landuse=cemetery|grave_yard`.
Pitch: `leisure=pitch|playground|sports_centre|stadium`. Field:
`landuse=allotments|orchard|vineyard|farmland|farmyard`. Park:
`leisure=park|garden`, `landuse=recreation_ground|village_green`. Grass:
`landuse=grass|meadow|flowerbed`, `natural=grassland`.
Closed ways and multipolygon outer rings are clipped to the
terrain extent and simplified at 0.5 m; areas below 4 m² and incomplete rings are
omitted. Outer member ways that fall outside the extract are fetched separately so a
multipolygon ring can still be closed. Inner rings are ignored. Polygons are
illustrative ground tints.

Not included: coastline and the fjord, and any land use OSM does not tag with the keys
above.

## Study area

The boundary is an authored rectangle for this demonstration. It is not a cadastral
parcel, property boundary, zoning designation or planning constraint.

## Simplifications

- Building footprints are simplified with a 1.2 m tolerance.
- Only building ways are included; multipolygon building relations are omitted.
- Heights come from `height`, then `building:levels`, then a typology fallback.
- Terrain is a coarse sampled heightfield, not an engineering surface.
- Buildings use one base elevation sampled at the footprint centroid; sloped
  footprints are not individually conformed to the terrain.

Generated by `scripts/build_site_packages.py` for the Terraform portfolio prototype.

## Building heights

© Kartverket. Licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Source: Høyde DOM1, the national surface model (1 m lidar), from the open NHM service https://wcs.geonorge.no/skwms1/wcs.hoyde-dom-nhm-25833, retrieved 2026-11-15.

`heights.json` is a separate layer keyed by OpenStreetMap building id; it is not merged into the ODbL footprint file. Building heights and simple gables measured from Kartverket's surface model (282 gabled, 161 flat, 77 other shapes drawn flat at their measured height); 0 not seen in the surface model keep the storey estimate. Not survey data.

Contains data under the Norwegian licence for Open Government data (NLOD) distributed by Statens vegvesen.
Licence: NLOD 1.0 (https://data.norge.no/nlod/en/1.0). Source: https://www.vegvesen.no/kart/ogc/norstoy_1_0/ows (layers Strategiskstoykart_lden and _lnight), retrieved 2026-11-15; calculation year 2021, read from the features.

Use limitation (publisher, verbatim): «Datasettet skal ikke brukes til å vurdere behov for tiltak på enkeltbygg. Det må utføres egne beregninger for dette.»

Road-noise bands are Statens vegvesen's strategic noise map (Lden and Lnight, calculated for 2021, CNOSSOS-EU), clipped to the terrain extent and simplified at 0.4 m; shown as published, not calculated here. The map is static: it does not respond to anything drawn in the studio.

## 1 m terrain

© Kartverket. Licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Source: Høyde DTM1 from the open NHM service https://wcs.geonorge.no/skwms1/wcs.hoyde-dtm-nhm-25833, retrieved 2026-11-16. Vertical datum NN2000.

1 m terrain is Kartverket DTM1 (bare earth, lidar), 624 × 734 cells over the terrain extent, stored in centimetres; 0 cells without data. Not a survey. Used for the section and slope tools; the drawn ground is still the package's coarser grid.

## Mapped hazards

Kilde: NVE (Flomsoner; Flom aktsomhetsområder; Aktsomhetskart for kvikkleireskred) and Contains data under the Norwegian licence for Open Government data (NLOD) distributed by Kartverket (Stormflo og havnivå).
Licence: Norsk lisens for offentlige data (NLOD), https://data.norge.no/nlod/. Source: Geonorge WFS services, retrieved 2026-11-16.

Flood zones: not mapped by NVE; Flood susceptibility areas: 3 mapped area(s) in the extent; Quick-clay susceptibility areas: 5 mapped area(s) in the extent; Storm surge, 200-year level in the 2100 climate: 1 mapped area(s) in the extent. "Not mapped" means the authority has no mapped area in the extent; it is not a statement that the site is safe. No depths, probabilities or safety classes are derived.

## Winter climate

Kilde: NVE. Licence: Norsk lisens for offentlige data (NLOD), https://data.norge.no/nlod/ (the publisher does not state a version). Source: NVE grid time series, https://gts.nve.no/api/GridTimeSeries (documented at https://api.nve.no/doc/gridtimeseries-data-gts/), theme `sd`, retrieved 2026-11-16.

Winter climate context is NVE's snow model on the seNorge grid (daily snow depth, 1 km cell at 22 m above sea level), winters 1990/91 to 2019/20; modelled for open terrain, not measured at the site. Not snowdrift, not snow accumulating on or around buildings, and not a snow load. Those need a specialist study.
